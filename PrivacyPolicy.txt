Neverwas privacy policy

Neverwas collects no data and sends no data anywhere.
- No crash or error reports are sent.
- No application ratings are fetched or uploaded.
- No usage statistics are collected or sent.

The only network request Neverwas makes on its own is the optional update check. It is off by default; when you turn it on, Neverwas asks GitHub for the latest release of https://github.com/ErickuchoSan/Neverwas and nothing else is sent.

Links you click (for example "search the web" or the project page) open in your web browser. What happens there is governed by the site you visit.

Neverwas is based on Bulk Crap Uninstaller by Marcin Szeniak (Apache License 2.0).
